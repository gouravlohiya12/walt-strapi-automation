# Landing Page Best Practices

Reference for the AI when generating page copy. Apply these patterns; don't reinvent.

## The anatomy of a high-converting page

A converting long-form landing page has 7 jobs, in order:

1. **Hook** — H1 + subhead. Promises the outcome they want, addresses the objection they fear.
2. **Pattern interrupt** — gold-standard / "wherever they learn first" — claims authority.
3. **Reframe** — "the talk is a myth" — destroys their old way of thinking.
4. **Reveal** — green intro banner — the new thing they need.
5. **Proof of system** — course intro + roadmap — shows it's organized, complete, real.
6. **Proof of trust** — guides + experts — real humans behind it.
7. **Risk reversal** — pricing + bonuses + FAQ — answers "why now, why this much, what if".
8. **Mobilizer** — final CTA — emotional, urgent.

Each section in the `raising-kids` template maps to one of these.

## Headline patterns that work

For H1:
- **"How to [outcome] without [common objection]"** — works for courses
- **"The [number]-step system to [outcome]"** — works for systems/methods
- **"[Benefit] for [specific audience]"** — works for tools/software
- **"[Unique transformation]"** — works for category-creators

For H1 sub (parens line):
- **"(Without the [pain1], [pain2], or [pain3])"** — addresses 3 objections
- **"(Even if [common excuse])"** — destroys the excuse upfront
- **"(In as little as [time period])"** — speed promise

## Body copy rules

- **Lead with pain** — name what they're experiencing, in their words
- **Sentences under 25 words** — anything longer breaks scanning
- **No marketing jargon** — "synergy", "leverage", "robust" are dead words
- **Concrete > abstract** — "save 10 hours a week" beats "boost productivity"
- **Show transformation** — before-state and after-state, side by side
- **Use "you" not "people"** — direct address always

## The 3-column "X is a Myth" pattern

Always 3 columns. Always in this order:
1. **Old Way:** what they do now (negative framing)
2. **New Way:** the alternative (your method)
3. **The Goal:** the outcome that justifies the change

Each ~25 words. Format every body line as if you're texting a friend who just complained about the old way.

## Roadmap section

8-10 numbered items. Each has:
- A number ("01.")
- A compelling question or outcome as title
- A duration in parens (only if real)
- A 1-line teaser of what they'll get

Patterns that work for titles:
- *"Episode N: How Do I [Real Question]?"*
- *"Episode N: What If [Worst Fear]?"*
- *"Step N: [Specific Outcome]"*
- *"Module N: The [System Name] Framework"*

The teaser should NOT just list topics. It should sell the takeaway.
- ❌ "Covers various aspects of marketing."
- ✅ "Why most marketing courses leave you with a hangover, and the one rule that fixes it."

## FAQ patterns

Address the 3-5 biggest objections, in this rough order:
1. **Am I qualified?** ("I'm not technical / experienced / smart enough")
2. **Will this work for me?** ("My situation is different / industry is weird")
3. **Risk question** ("What if it doesn't work / I don't have time")
4. **Money question** ("Is this worth $X / payment plan / refund")
5. **Logistics question** ("How long do I have access / format / device")

Each answer should:
- **Validate the concern** ("That's a real concern")
- **Reframe it** ("Here's what's actually true")
- **Reassure** with a specific detail (not vague)
- ~30-50 words max

## Pricing section anatomy

- **Total Value: $X (struck-through)** — the "regular" price
- **Your Price: $Y** — the actual price, always less than X
- **The savings is the offer** — "$X $Y today" framing creates urgency
- **3-4 bullets in the left column** — concrete deliverables (videos, docs, access duration)
- **Bonus column on right** — anything extra that increases perceived value
- **Italic statement under prices** — emotional anchor (mission, not feature)
- **Button below** — restates price in the action ("Start Today — $99")

## Social proof rules

For the "Meet Your Guides" + "Guest Experts" sections:

- **NEVER fabricate names or photos.** ASK the user.
- **Real bios > impressive bios** — credibility comes from specifics
- **A small photo with a real name beats a glamour shot with no proof**

If the client doesn't have any guests/credibility yet, hide those sections rather than fake them.

## Final CTA pattern

Two parts:
1. **Big light-weight headline** (font-weight 300/500) in yellow on white. Emotional, abstract. *"Your Silence Is An Answer."*
2. **Bold uppercase tagline below** in dark color. Imperative voice. *"STEP IN. BE THE SAFETY ANCHOR."*

The contrast in weight is the design move — light + bold side by side.

## Color rules

- **Yellow (#E9D985 / brand neutral)**: section backgrounds, accent text on dark, strikethrough emphasis
- **Green/Teal (#439A86)**: section backgrounds, "The Truth:" inline emphasis
- **Orange (#BF4E30)**: ALL CTAs, social icons, never anywhere else
- **Navy (#1F2833)**: headings, footer bg, body text
- **White**: main page bg, text on dark sections

Don't use orange for body text or section bg. Don't use yellow for body text (poor contrast). Don't use green for buttons (they should always be orange).

## Font weights

- **800** (extra bold) — only for the H1 and hero impact
- **700** (bold) — small accent headers, strong inline phrases
- **500** (medium) — section headlines (the user's preference based on Christian Sexuality reference)
- **400** (regular) — body text
- **300** (light) — final CTA "Your Silence Is" style

## Spacing rules

- **Sections**: 80-120px vertical padding (more on bigger sections like hero, pricing)
- **Headlines from body**: 24-40px gap
- **Inside cards**: 32px padding minimum
- **Mobile**: drop padding by ~50%, single column

## Mobile considerations

- All images responsive (max-width: 100%)
- Mobile font sizes (defined in template):
  - fs-72 → 48px
  - fs-96 → 55px
  - fs-48 → 30px
  - fs-36 → 24px
- Hamburger menu replaces full nav
- 2-column sections stack to single column

## What NOT to do

- ❌ "Lorem ipsum" placeholder copy in production
- ❌ Stock photos of "diverse smiling people in office" — feels generic
- ❌ More than 5 FAQs (overwhelms)
- ❌ Multiple CTAs with different actions (pick ONE primary action)
- ❌ Pop-ups or interrupts (this template doesn't have them; don't add them)
- ❌ Auto-playing video (kills mobile)
- ❌ Walls of text without visual breaks
